Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

India's 4 Wheel ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or app, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email, phone number, and password when you sign up.
  • Sell-car submissions: details about your car (brand, model, year, kilometres driven, fuel type) and contact details when you request a valuation or inspection.
  • Browsing activity: cars you view, save, or filter, to improve search results and recommendations.
  • Device information: browser type and IP address, used for security and basic analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide instant car valuations, loan eligibility estimates, and inspection bookings.
  • To contact you about your sell-car request, test drive, or account.
  • To show you saved cars and your submission history when logged in.
  • To improve our valuation accuracy and site performance.

3. How We Protect Your Information

Passwords are stored using one-way encryption (hashing) and are never visible to our team in plain text. Access to customer and seller data is restricted to authorised staff only. We use HTTPS encryption on all pages.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited details with our financing and inspection partners only when you explicitly request a loan offer or an inspection, and only to the extent needed to fulfil that request.

5. Your Choices

  • You can view and manage your saved cars and submissions from your account page.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

6. Cookies

We use browser local storage to remember your selected city and login session. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

7. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, reach us via the Contact Us page.
